\section{Conclusion}

BabyWhale demonstrates that the \emph{entire} modern LLM lifecycle can be
taught on one laptop-scale codebase, and that the teaching itself can be held
to the standard we hold code to: graded by tests, measured by commands, and
guarded against drift in CI. We offer it to the community both as a course
and as a template---the grader-versus-real-module mechanism and the
documentation drift guard transfer to any system that wants its explanations
to stay true.
